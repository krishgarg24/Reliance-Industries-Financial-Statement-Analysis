# Reliance-Industries-Financial-Statement-Analysis
5-year financial statement analysis of Reliance Industries using Excel

## 📊 What's Inside

- **Excel Financial Model** (`Reliance_Industries_5Y_Financial_Statement_Analysis_Project.xlsx`) — 4-sheet model:
  - `Raw Data` — sourced income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow figures
  - `Financial Statements` — fully formula-driven statements (EBITDA, EBIT, margins, equity, FCF)
  - `Ratio Analysis` — profitability, liquidity, leverage, and efficiency ratios
  - `Dashboard` — KPI cards, key ratio summary, and trend charts
- **Analysis Report** (`Reliance_Industries_5Y_Financial_Statement_Analysis_Report.pdf`) — 9-page written report: executive summary, statement-by-statement analysis, ratio analysis, and key insights

## 🔑 Key Findings (FY2022 → FY2026)

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2026 | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ₹6,94,673 Cr | ₹10,55,780 Cr | CAGR ~11.0% |
| EBITDA Margin | 15.6% | 17.0% | +140 bps |
| PAT | ₹67,845 Cr | ₹95,754 Cr | CAGR ~9.0% |
| ROE | 8.7% | 10.6% | +190 bps |
| Debt-to-Equity | 0.41x | 0.45x | Broadly stable |

**Highlights:**
- Revenue grew every year, driven by Jio Platforms and Reliance Retail alongside a resilient O2C (oil-to-chemicals) business
- Operating cash flow nearly doubled (₹1,10,654 Cr → ₹1,92,113 Cr), outpacing PAT growth — a sign of strong cash conversion
- Free cash flow turned solidly positive from FY2024 onward
- Leverage has remained moderate and broadly stable after a one-time spike in FY2023
- The FY2023 dip in reserves/equity reflects the 2023 demerger of Jio Financial Services, not an operating decline

## 🛠️ Methodology

- Data sourced from publicly available consolidated financial statements (screener.in, stockanalysis.com)
- All ratios and statement subtotals are calculated via live Excel formulas (not hardcoded)
- Free Cash Flow is approximated as CFO + CFI in the absence of a separately disclosed capex line
